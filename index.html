Blood Draw Volume

Estimate circulating blood volume and the proportion removed by diagnostic sampling. These are estimates, not patient-specific safe phlebotomy limits.

Default estimates: dog 80 mL/kg, cat 60 mL/kg. Adjust based on your institutional reference. Anemia, hemodynamic instability, and repeated sampling can substantially change what is appropriate.

Nutrition Support

Calculate resting energy requirements and generate an oral or tube-feeding schedule.

RER = 70 × weight(kg)^0.75. Verify the diet's actual caloric density. These calculations do not replace an individualized refeeding or nutrition plan.

Body Surface Area Calculator

Estimate body surface area for dogs and cats using a species-specific allometric equation.

Dog: 0.101 × kg^(2/3). Cat: 0.100 × kg^(2/3). Confirm the formula required by any medication-specific protocol before dose calculations.
